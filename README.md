## 👋 About Me

**Julián Laurito Riscica** — Senior Web & Marketing Automation Engineer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina 🇦🇷

I currently support web operations and marketing automation for a major global financial markets organization, working across Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Experience Manager, and DA.live. Before that, I led a four-person email development team at Accenture Song for a major international bank, and spent close to 8 years across email marketing, QA, campaign execution, and martech more broadly.

My work sits at the intersection of hands-on technical execution and marketing operations — planning, segmentation, QA, and governance, not just code. I'm also expanding further into modern frontend engineering (React, TypeScript, Vite) and edge/serverless platforms like Cloudflare Workers, and pursuing a Business Administration degree to sharpen the strategic side of how I approach martech.
